## Notes from class on this assignment
- the title, introduction, results, conclusion should all share the main conclusion from the paper
- repeating is good
- don't write it as a mystery story!
- the introduction is a map of the rest of the paper
  - background
  - what we did
  - methods
  - results
  - discussion

### Revising
- delete stuff that doesn't work anymore
- is each paragraph supporting the story?
- double-check references
- might have to update research questions
- re-write (check) introduction and abstract
- read on paper!

### Asking questions
1) data-first
   - theory (expectations of the data)
   - importance (how relevant)
   - availability (might need SQL or permissions)
   - iteration (might get multiple insights from a data set)
2) question-first
   - descriptive analysis
   - predictive analysis
   - inferential (can we explain the data?)
   - causal (what impact does x have on y?)
3) method-first (invent microscope and start looking!)

### Titles
- Descriptive of the study
- Examples he gave did not disclose the finding in the title
- "Exciting content: specific content" is another approach
- "A question? and an approach"

### Abstract - Rohan's recipe (sentences)
1) specify the general area of the paper and encourage the reader
2) secify the dataset and methods at a general level
3) specify the headline result
4) sentence about implications

### Introduction
- needs to be self-contained and convey everything that a reader needs to know
- rule of thumb: 10-15% of the whole paper
- same outline as described above
- should also telegraph the rest of the paper (section 2 will have x, section 3 will have y)
  - i.e. the general structure of the paper

### Data
- graphs that show all the data (or that represents the data)
- figure descriptions should be complete for the reader to understand without reading the paper
- how the data is generated (from city of toronto)
- for my paper, show all the data in a plot with the outlier and explain why it was removed (perhaps in the figure caption)

### Model
- optional for Donaldson paper

- data section can (should) contain the descriptive analysis

### Results
- communicate the outcomes
- don't include commentary (just the facts, maam)

### Discussion
- commentary, implications, opinions
- brief summary of the paper (results)
- key things we learned about the world
- section on weakness(es) or restrictions
- potential future work
